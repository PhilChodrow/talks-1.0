Abstracting Social Influence

Let \(x_i(t) \in \mathbb{R}\) be the opinion of agent \(i\) at time \(t\).

\[ \begin{aligned} \underbrace{\color{#d0bfff}{x_1}(t+1) - \color{#a5d8ff}{x_1}(t)}_{\text{opinion change } \delta_i(t+1)} = \overbrace{\lambda}^{\text{Learning rate}} \underbrace{\frac{\color{#fa5252}{x_2}(t) + \color{#ffc9c9}{x_3}(t)}{2}}_{\text{average opinion of neighbors}} \end{aligned} \]

More generally,




DeGroot (1974). “Reaching a consensus.” JASA






Let \(\partial i\) be the set of neighbors of agent \(i\).

\[ \begin{aligned} \underbrace{x_i(t+1) - x_i(t)}_{\text{opinion change } \delta_i(t+1)} &= \lambda \underbrace{\frac{1}{|\partial i|}\sum_{j \in \partial i} x_j(t)}_{\text{average opinion of neighbors}} \\ \end{aligned} \]

A realistic model?


















On a connected social graph where all agents listen (at least a little bit) to all their neighbors, DeGroot dynamics always converge to a consensus.

Generalizing with Influence Kernels

\[ \begin{aligned} \delta_i(t+1) &= x_i(t+1) - x_i(t) \\ &= \lambda \frac{1}{|\partial i|}\sum_{j \in \partial i} x_j(t) \quad \quad (\text{DeGroot}) \end{aligned} \]

\[ \begin{aligned} \delta_i(t+1) &= x_i(t+1) - x_i(t) \\ &= \lambda \frac{1}{|\partial i|} \sum_{j \in \partial i} \color{#A4303F}{K(\underbrace{ |x_j(t) - x_i(t)| }_{\text{unsigned distance } d_{ij}})} x_j(t) \end{aligned} \]




  • Attraction: \(K(d_{ij}) > 0\)
  • Repulsion: \(K(d_{ij}) < 0\)
  • Indifference: \(K(d_{ij}) = 0\)

We recover DeGroot dynamics if \(K(y) \equiv 1\).

There are other possible ways to normalize this

Kernel Zoo: DeGroot Dynamics


\[ \begin{aligned} \color{#A4303F}{k(d)} &= 1 \end{aligned} \]


\[ \begin{aligned} \delta_i = \lambda \frac{1}{|\partial i|} \sum_{j \in \partial i} \color{#A4303F}{k_\phi(d_{ij})} x_j \end{aligned} \]

Simulated on a random graph with a small amount of random noise incorporated into the update for interest.

Kernel Zoo: Bounded Confidence


\[ \begin{aligned} \color{#A4303F}{k_\phi(d)} &= \mathbb{1}[d < \phi] \end{aligned} \]


\[ \begin{aligned} \delta_i = \lambda \frac{1}{|\partial i|} \sum_{j \in \partial i} \color{#A4303F}{k_\phi(d_{ij})} x_j \end{aligned} \]

Creates sustained opinion clusters at approximately distance \(\phi\).

Hegselmann & Krause (2002). “Opinion dynamics and bounded confidence models, analysis, and simulation.” JASSS.

Deffuant et al. (2000). “Mixing beliefs among interacting agents.” Advances in Complex Systems.

Kernel Zoo: ✨Sigmoidal✨ Bounded Confidence


\[ \begin{aligned} \color{#A4303F}{k_\phi(d)} &= \left(1 + \exp \left[\phi_1(d^2 - \phi_0^2)\right]\right)^{-1} \end{aligned} \]


\[ \begin{aligned} \delta_i = \lambda \frac{1}{|\partial i|} \sum_{j \in \partial i} \color{#A4303F}{k_\phi(d_{ij})} x_j \end{aligned} \]

Converges to bounded-confidence dynamics with radius \(\phi_0\) as \(\phi_1 \rightarrow \infty\) and to DeGroot dynamics as \(\phi_1 \rightarrow 0\).

Allows linear stability analysis of bounded-confidence type behavior.

Brooks, PSC, Porter (2024). “Emergence of polarization in a sigmoidal bounded-confidence model of opinion dynamics.” SIADS.

Kernel Zoo: Smooth Attraction-Repulsion


\[ \begin{aligned} \color{#A4303F}{k_\phi(d)} = \left(1 - \frac{d^2}{\phi^2}\right) \exp\left(\frac{d^2}{-2\phi}\right) \end{aligned} \]


\[ \begin{aligned} \delta_i = \lambda \frac{1}{|\partial i|} \sum_{j \in \partial i} \color{#A4303F}{k_\phi(d_{ij})} x_j \end{aligned} \]

No standard attraction-repulsion kernel; we use a Ricker wavelet.

Generates close-knit communities that slowly diverge.

Ryan (1994). “Ricker, Ormsby, Klauder, Butterworth - A Choice of Wavelets.” CSEG Recorder.

How do we learn the kernel from data?



Easiest case: for each \(i\) there is only one neighbor \(j\).

\[ \begin{aligned} \delta_i \approx \lambda \color{#228be6}{k_\phi}(|\color{#fd7e14}{x_j} - \color{#fd7e14}{x_i}|) \color{#fd7e14}{x_j} \end{aligned} \]



Adams, White, and Araujo (2022). “Person-to-person opinion dynamics: An empirical study using an online game.” PLoS ONE.

A more complex case: multiple neighbors


\[ \begin{aligned} \delta_i &\approx \lambda \frac{1}{|\partial i|} \sum_{j \in \partial i} \color{#228be6}{k_\phi}(|\color{#fd7e14}{x_j} - \color{#fd7e14}{x_i}|) \color{#fd7e14}{x_j} \end{aligned} \]



Vande Kerckhove + al. (2016). “Modelling Influence and Opinion Evolution in Online Collective Behaviour.” PLoS ONE.
